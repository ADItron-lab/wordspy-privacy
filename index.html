Word Spy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Word Spy has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not sell your data. To play online we handle the nickname you type and your clues and messages during a game, and we delete them when the game room ends.

1. Who we are

Word Spy (“the app”, “the game”) is a word game for 3 to 5 players, made by Aditya Sali. You can contact us at aditronincs@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

  • Nickname: the name you type (up to 12 characters) when you create or join a room. Please do not use your real name or any personal information.
  • Game content: room codes, the clues you send, votes, and chat messages in private rooms. Public rooms have no chat.
  • Random player ID: a random code created on your device. It lets you rejoin your room if your phone sleeps or you switch apps. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the game and keep it secure.
  • Settings on your device: whether sound is on, which instruction screens you have seen, your last room code, and a saved copy of the game files so pass and play works offline.
  • Pass and play: this mode runs entirely on your device. Nothing from it is sent to us.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, payment details, or device advertising ID.

3. How we use it

  • To run game rooms and show your nickname, clues and messages to the other players in your room.
  • To let you reconnect to a game in progress.
  • To filter offensive words in nicknames, clues and messages.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

4. Who can see it

Other players in the same room can see your nickname, clues and chat messages. Public rooms can be joined by anyone, so strangers may see your nickname and clues there. We do not sell or share your information for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

Game data is held in the server’s memory only while a room is active. It is deleted shortly after the last player leaves, and whenever the server restarts. We do not keep a database of players or games. Our hosting provider may keep technical logs under its own policy. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

6. Other companies involved

  • Render hosts the game server and processes technical data such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game’s font, so your device contacts Google when the game loads.
  • Google Play distributes the app and has its own privacy policy.

We do not use advertising or analytics services.

7. Children

Word Spy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and you want something removed, contact us.

8. Security

The game connects over encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your nickname free of personal details.

9. Your choices

  • Clear the app’s storage or uninstall it to remove everything saved on your device.
  • Leave a room at any time. Its data is deleted shortly after the room empties.
  • Contact us to ask what we hold about you or to request deletion. Because there are no accounts, we may ask for your nickname and the time you played to find anything. Depending on where you live, you may have extra rights under local privacy law, and we will respect them.

10. Safety and reporting

Please be kind. Do not post offensive, hateful or unlawful content. Public room hosts can remove players from a lobby. To report a problem, email us at aditronincs@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, for example by adding accounts, ads or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top.
